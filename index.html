// about_me

Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Intelligent Manufacturing & Robotics (IRC IMR), King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia. My research focuses on intelligent manufacturing, robotics, and high-performance computing (HPC).

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Miskolc, Hungary, specializing in the intersection of language and social media for sentiment analysis. My expertise includes large language models, machine learning, and NLP. Previously, I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), Spain and contributed to AI-driven cybersecurity at the MYWAI Lab, University of Genoa, Italy.
