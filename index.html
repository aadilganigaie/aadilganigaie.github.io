About Me

Profile

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Intelligent Manufacturing & Robotics (IRC IMR), King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia — working at the intersection of AI, HPC, and industrial systems.

My PhD from the University of Miskolc focused on sentiment analysis and social media NLP. Post-doctorate I expanded into LLMs, AI Agents, cybersecurity for industrial devices, and cache-optimization with ML at UPV Spain. I have contributed to EU-funded research projects worth over €9.83M, published across Scopus-indexed journals, and collaborated with teams across Italy, Spain, Hungary, and Saudi Arabia.

My current research agenda spans LLM-driven automation, multimodal AI systems, industrial IoT security, and HPC performance tuning.
